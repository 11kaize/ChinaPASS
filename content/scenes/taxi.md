# Task

How to take a taxi in China using DiDi, from opening the app to arriving at your hotel.

---

# Before You Start

- [ ] Phone with internet (eSIM or local SIM)
- [ ] DiDi app installed, or Alipay → "Transport" / "Ride" mini-app
- [ ] Your destination **written in Chinese** (copy it from your booking)
- [ ] Alipay or WeChat Pay linked to a card

> 💡 **Tip:** Before you leave your hotel, ask the front desk to write the name and address of the hotel in Chinese and save it in your notes. You will need it every time you come back.

---

# Step-by-Step

1. **Open DiDi** (or Alipay → Transport → Ride).
2. **Switch to English** — tap the profile icon → Settings → Language → English.
3. **Enter your destination.** Paste the Chinese address. Do not type only "Hilton Hotel" in English — it may match the wrong city.
4. **Check the pickup point.** The app shows a green pin. Walk to it. If it is on the wrong side of the road, drag the map to adjust.
5. **Choose a ride type.** "Express" (快车) is the cheapest standard option. "Premier" is more expensive.
6. **Tap Confirm and wait.** The app shows the driver's plate number and a car model.
7. **Check the plate number before you get in.** Match the last 3–4 characters.
8. **Pay in the app.** Do not pay cash unless the driver asks. The fare is charged automatically after arrival.

---

# Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|
| Typing only the hotel name in English | Driver may go to the wrong place or cancel | Paste the Chinese address |
| Standing at the wrong exit | Driver cannot find you, you get charged a waiting fee | Check the pickup pin and the exit letter (A/B/C/D) |
| Not checking the plate number | You may get into the wrong car | Match the plate before entering |
| Canceling after the driver arrives | Cancellation fee applies | Cancel within the free window, or message the driver first |
| Not being able to describe yourself | Driver gives up and cancels | Use the bilingual card below |

# Useful Chinese Cards

Show these to the driver (full screen, large font):

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| I am at Exit B. | 我在 B 出口。 |
| I am wearing a black jacket. | 我穿着黑色外套。 |
| Please wait 2 minutes. | 请等我 2 分钟。 |
| I don't speak Chinese. | 我不会说中文。 |
| Please take me to this address. | 请带我去这个地址。 |
| Please stop here. | 请在这里停车。 |

**Full card to show the driver:**

> 你好，我不太会说中文。
> 我在 B 出口，穿黑色外套，请到这里接我。
> 目的地是：[中文地址]
> 谢谢！

# Plan B

If the ride fails or no driver accepts:

1. **Ask the hotel front desk to call a taxi for you.** Show them: 请帮我叫一辆出租车。
2. **Use the metro instead.** Most Chinese cities have English signage and ticket machines with an English option.
3. **Walk to a main road and hail a taxi.** Show the Chinese address on your phone through the window before getting in.
4. **Ask a staff member at a convenience store or mall information desk** to help you order.

---

# If Something Goes Wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Driver calls but you can't understand | Say "Hello, I don't speak Chinese" and send a text message in the app instead |
| You left something in the car | Open the order in DiDi → "Contact driver" / "Lost item" |
| You were charged too much | Open the order → "Feedback" → report the fare |
| Driver canceled | Just re-order; no action needed |
