# ChinaBuddy: an intelligent companion for everyday life in China

ChinaBuddy is a mobile companion for international visitors who are preparing to come to China, have just arrived, or are learning to navigate everyday life during their stay. Arriving in a new country involves more than finding attractions and planning an itinerary. Visitors also need to get connected, set up a practical payment method, reach their accommodation, communicate with staff, and understand unfamiliar digital services. These tasks can become difficult when instructions are scattered across different sources, local applications work differently from those at home, and language barriers make even a simple interaction uncertain.

Our project focuses on the transition from preparing for a trip to feeling capable of handling daily life independently. The first hours and days after arrival are a particularly important part of this experience: users may be tired, have limited connectivity, and need to complete several essential tasks before they can comfortably explore. ChinaBuddy continues to support them afterward through situations such as taking the metro, boarding a train, ordering food, checking into a hotel, and asking for help. The product is designed around the task a person needs to finish at that moment.

ChinaBuddy brings together three complementary forms of support: practical guides, ready-to-show Chinese communication cards, and an AI question-answering assistant. The guides break common situations into clear steps, explaining what users should prepare, what to do next, common mistakes, and alternative actions if the first approach does not work. Communication cards help users express an immediate need without having to compose or pronounce an unfamiliar sentence. Users can display Chinese text in a large, readable format, fill in relevant details, save useful cards, and create a personal destination card from an address they have already confirmed.

The AI assistant adds support when a user's situation does not fit a fixed guide. Before responding, it receives ChinaBuddy's purpose, the relevant bundled reference material, and the context the user chooses to share. This can include their interface language, manually selected city, journey stage, and preparation progress. The assistant can then explain the next step, offer a backup, or suggest a phrase to show someone nearby. Users configure their own supported model provider, and API credentials and conversations remain in the running application session. Personal address cards and payment or passport details are not automatically included in AI requests.

ChinaBuddy's distinctive focus is practical task completion across preparation, arrival, and daily adaptation. Its value is strongest in moments such as “I have landed, but I cannot get online,” “My payment did not work,” or “I need to tell the driver where to take me.” A task-based home screen, preparation checklist, clear guide navigation, and reusable Chinese cards help users move from uncertainty to a specific action. Relevant official source links also give them a route to check information that may change.

The current native demonstration includes nine everyday task guides, 96 communication cards, 207 offline phrases, a preparation checklist, city arrival notes, and AI chat with user-configured model access. The guides and cards are packaged with the application so they remain available without a network connection. Live AI answers require connectivity and a valid provider key. The demo provides guidance and communication tools; bookings, payments, and other service transactions are completed through the relevant providers.

Our goal is to reduce the time, effort, and uncertainty involved in getting started in China. We aim to make essential tasks easier to understand and complete, while helping users become more confident using local services. Future integrations could make transitions into relevant service applications more direct. These integrations should build on the same product principle: explain the task clearly, provide useful words when communication is needed, and help the user take the next practical step.
